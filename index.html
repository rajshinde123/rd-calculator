Understanding Recurring Deposits (RD)

What is a Recurring Deposit?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is an investment avenue offered by banks and the post office that lets you deposit a fixed sum of money every month for a pre-determined tenure at a fixed interest rate.

How is RD Interest Calculated in India?

According to RBI guidelines, interest on recurring deposits is compounded quarterly. The earlier monthly deposits stay invested longer and earn compound interest over more quarters than deposits made towards the end of tenure.

Quarterly Compounding Formula

M = P × (1 + r/400)4n/12
Where P is monthly deposit, r is annual interest rate (%), and n is number of months remaining.

Benefits of an RD

RDs cultivate disciplined monthly savings habits, offer guaranteed fixed returns unaffected by market fluctuations, and allow loan/overdraft facilities against the accumulated balance.
